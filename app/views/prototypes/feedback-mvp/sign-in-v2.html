Sign in to the Document Fraud Detection Service

This service highlights features of a document’s pages or file properties to help you find out if the file has been edited.

It does not indicate conclusively that fraud has taken place but provides signals to consider during your document review.

{{ govukButton({ text: "Sign in", href: "documents-none" }) }}

What you can check

  • bank statements
  • birth certificates
  • council tax documents
  • NHS letters
  • passports
  • school letters
  • tenancy agreements
  • utility bills
  • wage slips

This list is not exhaustive.

{{ govukInsetText({ text: "The service does not analyse content with third-party artificial intelligence (AI), understand what the words or images on the pages mean or compare results against other documents. " }) }}

If you feel further investigation is needed during your review, you may use the information the service provides to support your referral.
